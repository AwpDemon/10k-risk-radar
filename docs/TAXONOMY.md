# Risk taxonomy

Generated from `src/riskradar/taxonomy.py` (the single source of truth used by the prompts, the keyword baseline and the app).

| key | name | definition | includes | excludes / route elsewhere |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `cyber_tech` | Cyber & technology | Cyberattacks, data breaches, privacy/data-protection failures, IT system outages, technology change and digital disruption. | ransomware, hacking, information security, system failures, cloud/vendor IT outages, data privacy laws when the risk is about protecting data | AI/model-specific risks (-> ai_model); cyber *insurance claims* written by an insurer are underwriting exposure (-> reserving_underwriting) unless the text is about the company's own systems |
| `ai_model` | AI & model risk | Artificial intelligence, machine learning, generative AI, and reliance on models, algorithms, analytics or quantitative assumptions that may be wrong. | AI adoption/competition, AI regulation, model error, actuarial/pricing/credit models when the text's main point is that models or assumptions may be flawed | reserve adequacy where models are only mentioned in passing (-> reserving_underwriting) |
| `climate_catastrophe` | Climate & catastrophe | Natural and man-made catastrophes, severe weather, climate change (physical and transition risk), terrorism events, and ESG/climate-related scrutiny. | hurricanes, wildfires, earthquakes, convective storms, terrorism losses, climate regulation and ESG disclosure pressure | pandemics (-> pandemic_health) |
| `regulatory_legal` | Regulatory, legal & compliance | Laws, regulation, supervision, capital/solvency rules imposed by regulators, tax law, litigation, investigations, sanctions and compliance failures. | insurance/bank regulation, Dodd-Frank, Basel, NAIC, stress tests, consumer protection, AML/sanctions, lawsuits, tax changes, accounting standard changes | regulation that is specifically about AI (-> ai_model) or data privacy (-> cyber_tech) |
| `market_interest_rate` | Market & interest-rate risk | Changes in interest rates, credit spreads, equity markets, FX, and the resulting value of the investment portfolio or net interest income. | investment portfolio valuation, unrealized losses, low/high rate environment, hedging, LIBOR transition, inflation's effect on rates/asset values | borrower default (-> credit); broad recession narrative (-> macro_geopolitical) |
| `credit` | Credit & counterparty risk | Borrowers, issuers, reinsurers or other counterparties failing to pay; loan losses; allowance for credit losses; concentrations of credit exposure. | loan defaults, commercial real estate credit, consumer credit, counterparty exposure, investment defaults/impairments | reinsurer credit when the text is mainly about reinsurance availability (-> reinsurance) |
| `reserving_underwriting` | Reserving, pricing & underwriting | Insurance-specific risk that claims, benefits, mortality/morbidity, or losses exceed reserves and pricing; underwriting and actuarial assumptions; emerging claim issues. | loss reserve adequacy, social inflation, mass torts, asbestos, mortality/longevity, policyholder behavior, product guarantees, emerging coverage issues | catastrophe-driven losses (-> climate_catastrophe) |
| `liquidity_capital` | Liquidity, funding, capital & ratings | Ability to fund obligations, access capital markets, maintain capital levels, pay dividends from subsidiaries, and keep credit / financial-strength ratings. | deposit outflows, holding-company dependence on subsidiary dividends, ratings downgrades, debt covenants, collateral calls | regulator-imposed capital rules themselves (-> regulatory_legal) |
| `reinsurance` | Reinsurance | Availability, cost, adequacy and collectibility of reinsurance or retrocession. | reinsurance program limits, reinsurer insolvency, rising reinsurance prices | - |
| `pandemic_health` | Pandemic & public health | Pandemics (e.g. COVID-19) and other public-health crises and their business effects. | COVID-19 impacts on operations, claims, mortality, economy | - |
| `macro_geopolitical` | Macroeconomic & geopolitical | General economic conditions, recession, inflation, geopolitical conflict, political instability, government shutdowns, and country risk. | economic downturn, war (Russia/Ukraine, Middle East), trade policy, sovereign debt, political uncertainty | rate-specific effects (-> market_interest_rate) |
| `competition_strategy` | Competition, strategy & reputation | Competitive pressure, strategic execution, acquisitions/integration, new products, brand and reputational damage, distribution relationships, customer retention. | M&A integration, pricing competition, disintermediation, fintech/insurtech competitors, ESG reputation (when about reputation), client concentration | AI-driven competition (-> ai_model) |
| `operational_people` | Operational & people | Failures of processes, controls, third-party vendors, outsourcing, fraud, conduct, and dependence on key personnel / talent; business continuity. | key employees, talent retention, internal controls over financial reporting, third-party service providers, misconduct, operational errors | IT/cyber failures (-> cyber_tech) |
| `other` | Other / corporate structure | Stock-price, shareholder-rights, anti-takeover, governance, holding-company/foreign domicile structure, and accounting-estimate risks that fit nowhere else. | share price volatility, controlling shareholder, Bermuda/Irish domicile issues, goodwill impairment, critical accounting estimates | - |
